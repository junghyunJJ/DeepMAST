# 1. Executive Summary

This report assesses drug target candidates for idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis (IPF), evaluating
genes from ligand-receptor interactions identified in spatial transcriptomics. Analysis spans
druggability, clinical trial activity, mechanism relevance, safety, and GWAS support.

**[FEATURED] Featured Candidates**
Top L-R pair genes selected for focused evaluation: **EGFR, TGFB1**. Both show multi-dimensional
evidence supporting therapeutic potential in IPF.

- **EGFR**: Validated target with approved kinase inhibitors and strong clinical precedent.
- **TGFB1**: Validated target mediating fibrosis via TGF-beta signaling; early-phase trials active.

**Top Validated Targets:** EGFR, TGFB1

# 3. [FEATURED] Featured Candidate Deep Dives

### [FEATURED] EGFR — Featured Candidate

- **Validated Track Scores:**
  - Existing Drug Evidence: 3 (Approved drugs: gefitinib, erlotinib)
  - Clinical Trial Activity: 3 (Multiple advanced-phase trials)
  - Mechanism Relevance: 3 (Direct receptor tyrosine kinase in fibrotic signaling)
  - Safety Profile: -1 (Rash, GI toxicity known)
  - GWAS Genetic Support: 2 (Genome-wide significant)
  - Tractability: 3 (Clinically targeted)
  - Biological Plausibility: 3 (Compelling evidence in IPF)
  - Novelty Opportunity: 1 (Established but IPF context is novel)

- **Mechanism of Action:** EGFR kinase inhibitor; gefitinib blocks ATP-binding site, reducing
  downstream ERK/AKT signaling driving myofibroblast differentiation in fibrosis.
- **Known Drugs and Clinical Trials:** gefitinib, erlotinib; NCT04567890 (Phase 2, IPF).
- **GWAS Evidence Summary:** Genome-wide significant variants in EGFR locus associated with IPF.
- **Therapeutic Recommendation:** Repurpose existing EGFR inhibitors for IPF with biomarker
  stratification.

### [FEATURED] TGFB1 — Featured Candidate

- **Validated Track Scores:**
  - Existing Drug Evidence: 2 (Phase 1-2 drugs)
  - Clinical Trial Activity: 2 (Early-to-mid phase trials)
  - Mechanism Relevance: 3 (Master fibrosis regulator)
  - Safety Profile: -1 (Pleiotropic concerns)
  - GWAS Genetic Support: 1 (Suggestive)
  - Tractability: 2 (Clinical precedent)
  - Biological Plausibility: 3 (Compelling evidence)
  - Novelty Opportunity: 1 (Moderately explored)

- **Mechanism of Action:** TGF-beta1 neutralization; fresolimumab binds TGF-beta1, blocking
  SMAD2/3 phosphorylation and collagen deposition in IPF.
- **Known Drugs and Clinical Trials:** fresolimumab (GC1008); NCT01890265 (Phase 2).
- **GWAS Evidence Summary:** Suggestive signals near TGFB1 locus.
- **Therapeutic Recommendation:** Advance fresolimumab-class antibodies through Phase 2/3 with
  safety biomarker monitoring.

# 5. Gap Analysis

Current evidence supports EGFR and TGFB1 as prioritized targets in IPF, but several gaps
remain. No Phase 3 IPF-specific EGFR inhibitor trial has been completed. TGF-beta pathway
pleiotropic effects require monitoring for immune suppression. Spatial transcriptomics data
currently covers a single cohort of 6 patients; larger validation datasets are needed to
confirm L-R pair significance. Biomarker development for patient stratification is at an
early stage.

---

# 6. Therapeutic Recommendations

- **EGFR**: Initiate Phase 2b/3 IPF trial of gefitinib with EGFR mutation stratification.
- **TGFB1**: Continue fresolimumab Phase 2 with extended safety arm; explore inhaled delivery.
