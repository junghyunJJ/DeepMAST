# 4. Gap Analysis

BRCA1-targeting therapies show efficacy in BRCA1-mutant breast and ovarian cancer. IPF-specific
evidence for BRCA1 is limited. EGFR inhibitors need IPF-specific Phase 3 data. Larger spatial
transcriptomics cohorts are required.

---

# 5. Therapeutic Recommendations

- **BRCA1**: Explore PARP inhibitor repurposing in IPF with genomic stratification.
- **EGFR**: Initiate Phase 2b/3 IPF trial of gefitinib.
